Apna Editor: Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · हिंदी में पढ़ें

Your videos and projects stay on your device

Apna Editor edits videos on your phone or computer. Your projects, videos, photos, music and recordings are kept only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them, so save important projects as .avp files.

Using the app without an account

The Free plan works without an account. We do not collect your name, email or phone number for free use.

Ads (Free plan)

On Android, the Free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), the approximate location from the IP address, and app usage to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent first. See how Google uses information for ads. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Google › Ads). Pro has no ads. On the website and the Windows app, the Free plan shows only our own Pro card, which collects nothing.

Account (only to buy Pro or start the free trial)

You need an account only to start the free trial or buy Pro (email and password, or Google). This is what we keep, in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore):

  • Name and email: to sign you in, show your account and send messages about your account (email confirmation, password reset, payment receipts).
  • Mobile number: only if you type it in; used to contact you about your account or a payment.
  • Plan, Pro end date and free trial start: to turn Pro features on for you on every device.
  • Payment records (plan, amount, date, Razorpay or Google Play reference): to show your payments and for tax and accounting records.
  • Your choice about offer emails and the date you made it.

Passwords are handled by Firebase and are never visible to us. We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the services that run the app (Google Firebase, Razorpay or Google Play, Cloudflare for checking payments).

Offer and update emails

We send offers and news only if you tick "Email me offers and updates" when you sign up or in the Account screen. It is off unless you tick it. You can turn it off any time in the Account screen, or by writing to us.

Free trial

A new account gets Pro features free for a limited time (shown in the app). To give each email only one trial, we keep a one-way code made from your email (a SHA-256 hash, not the email itself), also after the account is deleted.

Changing or deleting your data

In the app, open Account to change your name and mobile number, turn offer emails on or off, or delete your account. Deleting it removes your name, email, mobile number and Pro time straight away and cannot be undone. Payment records are kept without your name or email for as long as tax law requires (in India, up to 8 years). You can also ask for a copy or deletion of your data by email.

Payments

On Android, Pro is bought through Google Play. On the website and Windows it is bought through Razorpay. Card, UPI and bank details go to Google Play or Razorpay, never to us. We receive only whether the payment succeeded, the plan and the amount.

Optional online features

Some tools you choose to use, such as AI translation or voices, YouTube upload, or a Local AI server you set up, send the text or media you pick to that service. Nothing is sent unless you use the tool.

Children

Apna Editor is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: pratimarakesh100k@gmail.com.

अकाउंट (सिर्फ फ्री ट्रायल या Pro के लिए): हम आपका नाम, ईमेल, मोबाइल नंबर (अगर आप डालें), प्लान, Pro की तारीख, ट्रायल शुरू होने की तारीख और पेमेंट का रिकॉर्ड Google Firebase में रखते हैं। ये सिर्फ साइन इन, Pro चालू करने, पेमेंट दिखाने और टैक्स रिकॉर्ड के लिए है। पासवर्ड हम कभी नहीं देख सकते।

हम आपका डेटा कभी बेचते नहीं हैं और ऐप चलाने वाली सेवाओं (Google Firebase, Razorpay/Google Play, Cloudflare) के अलावा किसी को नहीं देते।

ऑफ़र ईमेल: सिर्फ तभी भेजते हैं जब आप "Email me offers and updates" पर टिक करें। इसे कभी भी अकाउंट स्क्रीन से बंद कर सकते हैं।

फ्री ट्रायल: हर ईमेल को एक ही बार ट्रायल मिले, इसके लिए हम ईमेल से बना एक कोड (hash, ईमेल खुद नहीं) रखते हैं।
